What 36% means—and how the fixture got there

BROWSER FIXTURE · NO LEDGER · UNVALIDATED
Meaning

36 out of 100 probability mass—not “36% less energy”

For this generated fixture, the model assigns 36% probability to a lower-than-personal-usual energy/function check-in during the single hour from +72h to +73h after the forecast cutoff. It is not a diagnosis and does not guarantee that outcome.

Comparison

13 of 40 (32.5%) → 33.0% weighted → 36.4% posterior

Similarity and quality weighting add 0.5 points. The Beta(2,2) regularizing prior then pulls toward 50%, adding 3.4 points. The displayed 36% is rounded; neither step proves that a signal caused the outcome.

Why it landed here
  1. The cardio-autonomic feature is 7.5 and nearest the generated higher-deviation neighborhood (7.5).
  2. The sleep feature is −0.7 relative to its generated matched reference; it changes similarity weights but is not a causal explanation.
  3. Forty resolved synthetic cases pass prospective, schema, quality, identity and receipt gates.
  4. The completed check-in unlocks reveal; it does not recalculate or alter the sealed probability.
How it was calculated
  1. Freeze two feature values, quality, cutoff and provenance before the target window.
  2. Weight each eligible fixture by similarity × historical quality × current quality.
  3. Combine weighted outcomes with the named Beta(2,2) regularizing prior.
  4. Use a normal posterior approximation plus a quality penalty, then round bounds outward to 22–51%. This is an engineering band, not validated coverage.
What could change a future estimate
  1. New qualified features captured before a future cutoff.
  2. More prospectively resolved outcomes using the same endpoint and schema.
  3. Different quality, missingness or similarity to eligible history.
  4. A versioned model or policy change that passes offline review.
How this must improve
  1. Replace generated cases with consented and independently verified prospective outcomes.
  2. Measure held-out calibration, discrimination, subgroup behavior and interval coverage.
  3. Add sensitivity and ablation views showing which assumptions move the estimate.
  4. Retain abstention, human override, immutable cutoffs and ledger audits as release gates.
Similarity is not causality. These generated features cannot explain why a person feels a certain way, establish illness, or determine what will happen. The engineering band covers only posterior and fixture-quality uncertainty; model design error and real-world uncertainty are unmeasured. How you feel matters more than this score. This app cannot diagnose or rule out a medical condition.
